## What counts as commercial use?

Per the PolyForm Noncommercial license, a *permitted (free) purpose* is any
**noncommercial** purpose, plus personal use and use by noncommercial
organizations. Everything else — using CCM in or for a business, a paid product,
a client engagement, or a commercial service — is **commercial use** and needs a
commercial license.

## Getting a commercial license

Email **licensing@arib.sa** with:

1. Your company / legal entity name and country.
2. How you intend to use CCM (internal dev, in a product, client delivery, hosted service…).
3. Approximate team size / seats.

We will respond with terms and pricing. Typical structures we offer:

- **Team / seat license** — per-developer or per-team annual license.
- **Product / OEM license** — embed CCM in a commercial product you ship.
- **Enterprise license** — org-wide use, optional support and onboarding.

> Pricing is set per engagement at this stage; published tiers will follow.
