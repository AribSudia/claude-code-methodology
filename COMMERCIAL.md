## What counts as commercial use?

Per the PolyForm Noncommercial license, a *permitted (free) purpose* is any
**noncommercial** purpose, plus personal use and use by noncommercial
organizations. Everything else — using CCM in or for a business, a paid product,
a client engagement, or a commercial service — is **commercial use** and needs a
commercial license.

### Examples (free vs. paid)

| Scenario | Result |
|----------|--------|
| You use CCM for personal study, a hobby project, or academic research | **Free** |
| A charity, school/university, public-research, or government body uses CCM (regardless of funding source) | **Free** |
| You're **evaluating** CCM to decide whether to buy — even inside a company | **Free during a 30-day evaluation** (see below) |
| A **freelancer/consultant** uses CCM to deliver paid client work | **Paid** |
| A **for-profit company** uses CCM for internal tooling that is never sold | **Paid** |
| CCM is embedded in, or used to build, a **product or service you sell** | **Paid** |
